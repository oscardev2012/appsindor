Questions, answered clearly

Before you schedule

What is included in the $350 Roof Tune-Up?+

It is a general annual maintenance visit for accessible roof areas. The planned scope includes a visual roof review, maintenance caulking or sealant at exposed fasteners and accessible roof penetrations, chimney water-entry details, and a review for visible roof concerns. It does not include roof replacement, hidden-condition repairs, structural work, decking, major repairs, interior repairs, or items requiring a separate scope.

What is the emergency tarp price?+

The published starting emergency tarp service is $125 for one tarp up to 20 × 20 feet, subject to a safe site assessment. A larger standard tarp is $150. Multiple tarps, whole-roof coverage, unusual access, unsafe weather, steep roofs, or additional materials require a separate confirmation before work begins.

Can Majestic help with my insurance claim?+

Majestic can inspect visible conditions, document the roofing project, prepare a roofing estimate or scope, and meet at the property with an adjuster when requested. Majestic does not decide coverage, guarantee claim approval, or act as a public adjuster unless separately licensed and authorized.

Is the price planner a final quote?+

No. It is an early planning range based on the information you provide. Roof geometry, measurements, materials, access, code requirements, permits, decking, and hidden conditions are confirmed in person.

What cities do you serve?+

Majestic serves properties approximately 90 minutes from Charlotte, NC or Rock Hill, SC. Roof and claim-related inspections do not have a travel fee. Availability is confirmed by address, and Raleigh is outside the service area.
