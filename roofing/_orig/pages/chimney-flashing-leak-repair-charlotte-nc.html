What you may notice

Visible clues help define the inspection—not the final diagnosis.

Use these examples to describe what you see from a safe location. A photo cannot confirm every roof layer, hidden moisture, cause or repairability.

  • Stain beside the chimney after rain
  • Loose, rusted or lifted metal flashing
  • Cracked sealant at counterflashing
  • Debris or standing water behind the chimney
  • Damaged shingles or underlayment at sidewalls

What happens when we arrive

Six checkpoints from arrival through closeout.

Majestic documents the roof as a construction system, explains choices before work begins and keeps insurance coverage decisions separate from contractor work.

01

Arrival, interview and safety check

The technician confirms what happened, when the condition was first noticed, whether water is entering now and which interior rooms are affected. Before using a ladder, Majestic checks weather, electrical, tree, structural and fall hazards and explains any access limitation.

02

Interior and ground-level reference

When relevant and accessible, the team starts at the stain, drip or impacted room, reviews the attic from a safe access point and then walks the exterior perimeter. This creates a reference line between the interior evidence and the roof area above it.

03

Roof-system inspection

When safe access is available, Majestic reviews affected slopes plus shingles, ridge and hip caps, valleys, flashing, pipe boots, vents, skylights, roof edges, gutters, fascia and soffit. Wide photographs show location; detail photographs show the condition.

04

Measurements and factual documentation

The record identifies visible conditions, roof geometry, affected components and estimated quantities. Storm-related observations are separated from age, maintenance, installation details and hidden conditions that cannot be confirmed without authorized removal.

05

Options explained before authorization

Majestic explains immediate protection, localized repair, section rebuild and replacement paths when applicable. The homeowner receives a written roofing path and can choose an out-of-pocket project or use the factual documents in a claim they manage with their carrier.

06

Construction, verification and closeout

After a written scope is approved, materials and schedule are confirmed. Majestic performs the authorized roofing work, controls debris and cleanup, photographs completion and provides the applicable invoice, warranty and project-closeout documents.

Repair options

The inspection determines which option can be built reliably.

Final materials, quantities, code requirements and hidden conditions are confirmed in the written scope.

Targeted flashing repair

Replace an isolated failed piece when surrounding shingles, underlayment and metal remain compatible.

Flashing rebuild

Remove adjacent roofing and reconstruct apron, step and counterflashing details as the assembly requires.

Cricket or back-pan work

Improve upslope water diversion where chimney width and roof geometry require it, subject to code and design review.

Masonry coordination

A cracked crown, open mortar joint, brick condition or cap problem is referred or coordinated with the appropriate chimney/masonry trade.
