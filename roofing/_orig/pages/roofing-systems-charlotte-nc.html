Available project paths

Six roofing categories Majestic can evaluate.

Product availability and eligibility are confirmed in writing for the individual property. Current manufacturer names and exact Class 4 lines will be published after final company confirmation.

01

Architectural asphalt shingles

Dimensional shingles for residential slopes, installed as a complete system with starter, underlayment, ridge, ventilation and flashing details.

02

Class 4 impact-resistant options

Products carrying a current Class 4 impact-resistance classification can be quoted by exact manufacturer and line. Classification does not mean impact-proof and does not promise an insurance discount or claim result.

03

Designer and premium shingles

Heavier profiles and distinctive patterns may be available when the roof structure, slope, budget and current product specifications are appropriate.

04

Metal roofing

Standing-seam and other metal options are evaluated for substrate, transitions, penetrations, ventilation, thermal movement and manufacturer requirements.

05

Low-slope and flat roofing

The selected membrane or coating depends on drainage, existing assembly, penetrations, insulation, deck condition and commercial or residential use.

06

Commercial roof systems

Inspection, leak investigation, maintenance, repairs and replacement scopes for qualifying retail, restaurant, office, warehouse, multifamily, church and development projects.
