What Is a Roofing Contractor Supplement?

A supplement is a factual request to review construction scope, quantities, materials or documented conditions—not a promise that the carrier will approve or pay an item.

Factual roofing information for homeowners in Charlotte, NC and the surrounding Carolinas.

01

Why a scope may differ

  • Roof measurements or waste calculations differ.
  • A slope, valley, ridge, flashing detail or related component is absent from the carrier estimate.
  • Product availability or manufacturer installation requirements affect the physical work.
  • Permit or applicable code requirements must be documented for construction.
  • Hidden decking or structural conditions become visible after tear-off and require written change documentation.
02

What Majestic prepares

  • A clear description of the construction item under review.
  • Roof measurements, quantities and affected locations.
  • Photographs and field notes supporting the visible condition.
  • Product, manufacturer or code information when relevant to construction.
  • An updated written contractor estimate or scope with supporting documents.
03

What happens next

  • The homeowner and carrier receive or exchange the requested documents through the authorized process.
  • The carrier reviews the information and independently decides whether its estimate or claim decision changes.
  • Majestic can answer factual questions about measurements, materials and construction scope.
  • Majestic does not interpret policy coverage or guarantee acceptance.
  • Any approved or homeowner-authorized construction change is documented in writing before the additional work proceeds.
