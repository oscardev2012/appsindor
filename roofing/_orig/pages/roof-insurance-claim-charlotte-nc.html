Five documented steps

From storm concern to completed roof.

Each step has a defined owner, record and decision point. The homeowner and carrier manage the insurance claim; Majestic manages the roofing evidence and construction scope.

01

Protect people and the property

If a tree, open hole or active leak creates an immediate hazard, the first call may need to be 911, the utility, fire department or a qualified tree/structural professional. Majestic then confirms whether roof access is safe. When weather and conditions permit, temporary tarping may help limit additional water entry; the homeowner should preserve time-stamped photos, invoices and receipts.

02

Inspect the roof as a complete system

At the appointment, the technician first asks what happened, when it was noticed and which rooms are affected. The inspection can connect safe interior or attic evidence with the exterior roof area, then review accessible slopes, shingles, ridge and hip caps, valleys, flashing, pipe boots, vents, skylights, gutters, fascia, soffit and visible decking. Unsafe or inaccessible areas are clearly noted.

03

Build a factual construction record

Majestic organizes wide-location photos, detail photos, notes, estimated measurements, roof geometry, affected components, material observations and the proposed repair or replacement scope. Visible storm patterns are separated from age, maintenance, installation details and hidden conditions. The record explains physical roofing work without interpreting the insurance policy.

04

Prepare for and coordinate the adjuster visit

The homeowner reports the event, opens the claim and schedules the carrier’s adjuster. With homeowner authorization and a confirmed appointment, Eric or another Majestic representative may attend the property to identify the same documented roof locations, answer technical construction questions and explain the contractor estimate. The carrier independently evaluates cause, coverage and payment.

05

Resolve construction scope, build and close out

Majestic compares its roofing scope with the available carrier estimate. If documented quantities or necessary construction components differ, the company may submit a factual contractor supplement with measurements, photos and supporting construction details. After the homeowner approves the written agreement, Majestic schedules materials and installation, completes cleanup and provides final photos, invoice, applicable warranty and requested completion documents.

What happens at the property

What the free inspection appointment includes.

The goal is a clear, repeatable inspection—not a rushed photo from one roof spot. Safe access and property conditions determine which observations can be completed.

01

Ten-minute roof interview

We record the event, discovery time, weather conditions, active leaks, prior repairs and the rooms or exterior sides involved.

02

Safety and access plan

The technician identifies electrical, tree, structural, surface, slope and weather risks before choosing ground, ladder, roof, attic or drone viewpoints.

03

Context before close-ups

Overview photos establish each slope and roof feature. Detail images then document the exact location and visible condition without relying on isolated marks.

04

Component-by-component review

Shingles, caps, valleys, flashing, penetrations, ventilation, decking indicators and attached drainage components are reviewed as one system.

05

Same-day explanation

Majestic explains what was observed, what remains unknown, whether temporary work is recommended and the next repair, replacement or documentation step.

06

Written handoff

The homeowner receives available photos, report, estimated measurements and the written roofing path so the next decision is based on the same factual record.

FAQ

Roof insurance questions, answered carefully.

Is the roof inspection free?+

Yes. Majestic offers a free roof inspection for suspected storm damage, with no obligation. Availability depends on location, weather and safe access.

Does Majestic file the insurance claim for me?+

No. The homeowner reports accurate facts and files the claim with the insurance carrier. Majestic can provide factual roof documentation, measurements and a written contractor estimate.

What are ACV, RCV and depreciation?+

They are insurance-policy and payment terms. Their definitions and application depend on the specific policy and carrier. Ask the insurer or a licensed insurance professional for an interpretation. Majestic can provide roofing completion documents when requested.

What is a roofing supplement?+

A contractor supplement is a factual request for review of construction scope, quantities, materials or documented conditions that may differ from the carrier estimate. It is not a guarantee of coverage or payment.

Can Majestic meet the adjuster?+

When requested by the homeowner, scheduled and permitted, a Majestic representative may attend to point out documented conditions and explain the written contractor scope. The carrier independently evaluates the claim.

Do you work with my insurance company?+

Majestic uses a carrier-neutral documentation process and has experience on roofing projects involving State Farm, Allstate, Liberty Mutual and other carriers. Majestic is not affiliated with or endorsed by any insurer.

What warranty applies?+

Qualifying roof replacements include a 10-year Majestic workmanship warranty. Manufacturer warranty terms depend on the selected and documented roofing product.
