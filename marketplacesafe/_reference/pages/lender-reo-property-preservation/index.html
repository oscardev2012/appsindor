North Carolina WDIR

WDIR report and contractor repair are different assignments.

In North Carolina, a WDIR (Wood-Destroying Insect Information Report) must be completed by a licensed structural pest professional. It reports visible evidence in accessible areas; it is not a structural damage report. SAFE does not issue the WDIR. We can review the repair items that follow, prepare the construction scope, coordinate qualified specialists, complete authorized repairs, and provide repair documentation for the transaction file.
